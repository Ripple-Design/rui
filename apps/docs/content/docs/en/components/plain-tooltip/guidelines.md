## Usage guidance

Use `RPlainTooltip` for short supporting hints that clarify the purpose of an existing control.

- Keep the copy brief and descriptive.
- Do not rely on the tooltip as the only accessible label for icon-only controls.
- Prefer popover or menu patterns when the floating content needs interaction or multiple actions.
